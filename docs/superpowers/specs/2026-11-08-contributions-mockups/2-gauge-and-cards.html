Saving tab: the gauge and tidier contribution cards

Choosing Saving swaps the top gauge, the same way Spending has "Left to spend". Click the gauge you prefer and the card style you prefer, then tell me in the terminal.

1 · Which gauge?

A gauge showing only this month's fund money would always be full, because funds are added in full when you confirm the month. A uses your funds' running totals instead. B counts everything put away this month.

A · Funds in the gauge
The gauge is the running total of all your funds against their targets (like today's "Total saved"), with this month's +$750 under it. Contributions are the text pill, which is the "still nice in the UI" part you asked for. Tapping the pill opens the month's breakdown (screen 3).

B · This month in the gauge
The gauge is everything put away this month: funds count when you confirm, contributions count once deposited. It starts the month around a quarter lit and fills as you log deposits. This includes contributions, which you said you didn't need, so it's here only to compare against A.
